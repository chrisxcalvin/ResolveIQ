---
title: How to Update Your Account Information
category: general_query
---

Most account details — name, email, phone number, and mailing address —
can be updated directly in the app under Account Settings, and changes to
email or phone require confirming a one-time code sent to the new
contact method.

Legal name changes (e.g. after marriage) require a supporting document
upload for verification and cannot be self-serviced, since the name on
file must match the identity documents used during account verification.

If a customer no longer has access to their registered email or phone,
they cannot use self-service and must go through identity verification
with a support agent before any contact information can be changed.
