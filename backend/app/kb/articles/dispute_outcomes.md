---
title: Dispute Outcomes — What Happens If You Win or Lose
category: dispute
---

If a dispute is decided in the customer's favor, any provisional credit
becomes permanent and no further action is needed. If it is decided
against the customer, the provisional credit (if one was issued) is
reversed and the original charge stands.

Card network decisions on disputes are generally final — there is
typically no further appeal available through us once a network has
ruled, though in rare cases new evidence can prompt a re-investigation.

Customers should be told the outcome clearly, including the specific
reason given by the network when one is available, rather than just
"the dispute was denied," since a vague answer tends to generate a
follow-up complaint.
