---
title: Late Fees and How to Dispute Them
category: general_query
---

A late fee is charged when a payment is not received by its due date,
distinct from the other fee types covered elsewhere (overdraft, foreign
transaction, maintenance) — it is specifically about timing, not the
amount or nature of a transaction.

Most account agreements allow one late-fee waiver as a courtesy for an
otherwise good payment history, typically once every twelve months. A
customer disputing a late fee should be asked whether the payment was
actually submitted before the due date (a timing or processing-delay
issue) or simply submitted late, since the two cases are handled
differently — the former may point to a payment-failure issue rather than
a fee dispute at all.

Repeated late fees on the same account can indicate a recurring payment
or autopay setup problem worth flagging separately, rather than waiving
each fee individually without addressing the underlying cause.
