---
title: Standard Refund Processing Timeline
category: refund_delay
---

Once a refund is approved, we release the funds back to the original
payment method within 1 business day on our side. From there, how long it
takes to actually appear depends on the customer's bank or card issuer —
typically 5-10 business days for card refunds, and 3-5 business days for
bank transfers.

This means a customer may see the refund confirmed in our app before it
appears in their bank account or statement — that gap is normal and
expected, not a sign that anything went wrong.

If a refund was approved more than 10 business days ago and still has not
appeared, that is outside the normal window and should be escalated for
investigation.
