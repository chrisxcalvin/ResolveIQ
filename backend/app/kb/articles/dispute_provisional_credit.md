---
title: Provisional Credit During a Dispute
category: dispute
---

For many dispute types, we issue a provisional credit for the disputed
amount while the investigation is ongoing, so the customer is not out of
funds during the 30-45 day process.

A provisional credit is temporary, not final — if the dispute is
ultimately decided in the merchant's favor, the provisional credit is
reversed and the charge stands. Customers should understand this is
conditional before they treat the funds as permanently theirs.

Not every dispute qualifies for a provisional credit — high-risk or
unclear cases may wait for a final decision before any funds move.
