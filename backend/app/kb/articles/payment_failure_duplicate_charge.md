---
title: Troubleshooting a Duplicate or Double Charge
category: payment_failure
---

Duplicate charges usually happen when a customer's browser or app
resubmits a payment after a slow network response — the customer sees a
loading spinner, assumes it failed, and taps pay again while the first
request is still processing.

The first step is always to check whether both charges actually settled,
or whether one is still a pending authorization hold that has not been
captured yet — pending holds are not real charges and will expire on
their own within 3-5 business days without any action needed.

If both charges genuinely settled, the duplicate should be refunded in
full within 2 business days. Escalate to a specialist if the duplicate
charge is not visibly reversed within that window.
