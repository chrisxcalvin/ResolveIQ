---
title: Payment Taken for the Wrong Amount or on the Wrong Date
category: payment_failure
---

This is distinct from a duplicate charge: here, a single payment was
processed, but for an amount or on a date that does not match what the
customer authorized — not an extra charge, but a wrong one.

A wrong amount usually traces back to one of two causes: a merchant-side
billing error (the amount charged doesn't match the agreed price), or a
currency/rounding conversion on a cross-border payment. A wrong date
usually means a scheduled or recurring payment ran on its configured
billing date rather than the date the customer expected it to.

Ask the customer for the exact amount and date they expected versus what
was actually charged — this distinguishes a system timing issue (the
payment ran on its correct scheduled date, just earlier than the customer
remembered) from a genuine processing error (the payment ran on a date or
for an amount that doesn't match its own schedule or agreement). Only the
latter needs correcting; the former is a case of resetting the customer's
expectation, not the transaction.
