---
title: Understanding Your Statement and Fees
category: general_query
---

Monthly statements are generated on the same calendar day each month
based on the account's original opening date, and are available in the
app under Statements as well as by email if statement notifications are
enabled.

Common fee line items include a monthly maintenance fee (waived if a
minimum balance or activity threshold is met), foreign transaction fees
on non-domestic purchases, and occasional one-time fees such as
expedited card replacement.

If a customer disputes a fee they believe was charged in error, check
whether a waiver condition was actually met before assuming the fee is
correct — misapplied waivers are a common source of billing questions.
