---
title: Refund to Original Payment Method vs. Statement Credit
category: refund_delay
---

By policy, refunds are always issued back to the original payment method
used for the purchase — we cannot redirect a refund to a different card,
bank account, or as store credit unless the original method no longer
exists (e.g. the card was cancelled).

This is a card network requirement, not an internal policy choice, so it
cannot be overridden case-by-case even if the customer would prefer a
different method.

If the original method truly no longer exists, the refund is issued as a
statement credit or, for bank transfers, requires the customer to confirm
a new destination account before it can be released.
