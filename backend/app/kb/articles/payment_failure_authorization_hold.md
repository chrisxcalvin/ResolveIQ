---
title: Payment Failed But Funds Are Still Held
category: payment_failure
---

A customer may report that a payment "failed" in our app but their bank
still shows funds as unavailable. This happens because the payment
attempt placed a temporary authorization hold with the bank before the
transaction was ultimately declined or reversed on our side.

Authorization holds are not actual charges — no money has moved. Holds
are released automatically by the issuing bank, typically within 3-5
business days, though the exact timing is controlled by the bank, not by
us.

We cannot force a bank to release a hold early. If it has been longer
than 7 business days and the hold is still showing, the customer should
contact their bank directly, since only the bank can manually release it.
