---
title: Lost or Stolen Refund — What To Do
category: refund_delay
---

This is different from a refund simply running late: here, the refund was
issued and should have arrived, but the customer never received it at all
— the money or instrument went missing in transit rather than just taking
longer than the standard timeline.

For a refund sent as a physical check or money order, ask whether it was
ever received and whether it has since been cashed by someone else —
these point to different next steps (a replacement reissue versus a
fraud investigation). For a refund sent electronically to the original
payment method, "lost" almost always means it was misdirected to a closed
or incorrect account/card, not that it vanished in transit, so confirm
the destination account or card is still open and correctly on file
before assuming it is truly lost.

Never reissue a lost refund before confirming the original was not
already cashed or deposited elsewhere — doing so risks paying the same
refund twice. A reissue should be placed on hold pending that
confirmation, not processed immediately on the customer's say-so alone.
