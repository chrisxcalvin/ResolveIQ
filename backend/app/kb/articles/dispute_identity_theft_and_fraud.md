---
title: Identity Theft and Fraud — What To Do
category: dispute
---

Identity theft and fraud are handled differently from an ordinary billing
dispute. A billing dispute contests one transaction a customer recognizes
but disagrees with; fraud means the customer's card, account, or identity
was used by someone else without authorization at all.

If a customer reports fraud or suspects identity theft, their card or
account access should be suspended immediately to stop further
unauthorized use, before the investigation into any individual
transaction even begins. This is a security action, separate from — and
faster than — the standard dispute-investigation timeline.

Customers should also be advised to place a fraud alert or credit freeze
with the major credit bureaus and to review their recent statements for
any other unrecognized activity, since fraud rarely affects only one
transaction. A police report is not required to open an internal fraud
investigation, but it strengthens the customer's position if the case
later involves a merchant or bureau dispute.

Funds lost to confirmed unauthorized fraud are typically restored faster
and more fully than a standard merchant dispute, since the customer bears
no responsibility for transactions they did not authorize.
