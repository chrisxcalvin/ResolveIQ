---
title: How to Unlock Your Account — Self-Service Steps
category: account_lock
---

For locks caused by failed login attempts or a new-device flag, the
customer can usually self-unlock by completing identity verification in
the app: confirming their registered email or phone via a one-time code,
and confirming their date of birth.

Self-service unlock is only available for low-risk locks. If the account
was locked due to a suspected fraud signal or a compliance review, the
self-service flow will not be offered, and the account must be manually
reviewed before it can be unlocked.

Customers should never be told to share a one-time verification code with
support — that code is meant to stay between the customer and the app.
