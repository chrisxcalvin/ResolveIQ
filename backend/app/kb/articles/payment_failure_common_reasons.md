---
title: Why Payments Fail — Common Reasons
category: payment_failure
---

Most failed payments happen for one of four reasons: insufficient funds in
the linked account, an expired or incorrectly entered card, the issuing
bank declining the transaction for its own risk reasons, or our fraud
system flagging the transaction for manual review.

When a payment fails, the customer is not charged — no funds are moved
until a transaction is fully authorized and settled. If a customer reports
seeing a pending charge for a failed payment, that is an authorization
hold, not an actual charge, and it will automatically drop off within 3-5
business days.

If the decline reason returned by the bank is generic ("do not honor"),
we cannot see the bank's internal reasoning — the customer should contact
their card issuer directly to find out why the transaction was declined.
