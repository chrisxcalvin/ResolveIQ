---
title: How to Contact Support and Expected Response Times
category: general_query
---

Support is available through in-app chat, email, and social channels, all
of which feed into the same ticket queue so a customer's history is
visible regardless of which channel they used previously.

Standard response time is within 1 business day for routine queries.
High-urgency categories — suspected fraud, account lockouts, and large
disputed transactions — are prioritized ahead of routine queries and
typically receive a first response much faster.

Customers do not need to repeat their issue across multiple channels to
get a faster response — doing so creates duplicate tickets and can
actually slow things down, since duplicate tickets have to be manually
merged.
