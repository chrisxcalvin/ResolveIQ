---
title: Preventing Future Account Lockouts
category: account_lock
---

The most common preventable cause of lockouts is a saved, outdated
password being auto-filled by a browser or password manager after the
customer changed their password elsewhere. Encouraging customers to
update their saved password in their browser after any password change
prevents most repeat lockouts.

Enabling two-factor authentication also reduces lockouts caused by
new-device flags, since a verified second factor lets our system trust a
new device more quickly instead of flagging it as risky.

Keeping the registered email and phone number up to date is the single
most important thing a customer can do, since both self-service unlock
and account recovery depend on being able to reach the customer through
one of those channels.
