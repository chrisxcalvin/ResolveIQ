---
title: Common Reasons Accounts Get Locked
category: account_lock
---

Accounts are locked automatically for three main reasons: five or more
consecutive failed login attempts, login activity from an unrecognized
device or location combined with other risk signals, or a routine
compliance/KYC review being triggered on the account.

A lock is a protective measure, not a punishment — it exists to stop a
potential unauthorized access attempt while it can still be investigated,
not because the customer did anything wrong.

The reason for a specific lock is visible in the account's internal flag
history and should always be checked before telling a customer why their
account was locked, since giving the wrong reason causes unnecessary
alarm.
