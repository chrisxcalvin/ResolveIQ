---
title: Dispute Investigation Timeline and Process
category: dispute
---

Once filed, a dispute is submitted to the card network, which gives the
merchant a chance to respond with evidence. This process typically takes
30-45 days end to end, though simple, clear-cut cases can resolve faster.

During the investigation, the customer may be asked for additional
documentation — receipts, correspondence with the merchant, or proof the
service was not delivered. Delays are most often caused by the customer
not responding to these requests promptly.

Support agents should set the expectation up front that disputes are not
resolved instantly, since customers who expect an immediate outcome tend
to follow up repeatedly and unnecessarily during the standard window.
