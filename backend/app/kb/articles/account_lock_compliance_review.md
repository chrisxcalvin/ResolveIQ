---
title: Account Locked for Compliance/KYC Review
category: account_lock
---

Some locks are triggered by routine compliance requirements rather than
anything suspicious about the customer specifically — for example,
periodic re-verification of identity documents, or a transaction pattern
that automatically requires a manual compliance check.

These reviews are handled by a dedicated compliance team, not by general
support, and typically take 1-3 business days. Support agents cannot
expedite a compliance review or unlock the account directly, even if the
customer is frustrated by the wait.

The customer should be told honestly that this is a standard review, an
estimated timeframe, and that no further action is needed from them
unless compliance reaches out requesting documents.
