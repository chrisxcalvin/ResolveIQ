---
title: How to Check Refund Status
category: refund_delay
---

Every refund has one of three states: pending (approved, not yet sent to
the bank), sent (released to the bank, awaiting posting), or completed
(confirmed posted by the bank).

A "sent" status means the money has already left our system — at that
point the delay, if any, is entirely on the customer's bank, and we no
longer have visibility into exactly when it will post to their account.

Customers can be told the current status and the date it moved to that
status, but we cannot promise an exact arrival date once a refund is in
the "sent" state, since that timing is controlled by the receiving bank.
