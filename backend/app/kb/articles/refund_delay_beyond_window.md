---
title: Why a Refund Might Be Delayed Beyond the Standard Window
category: refund_delay
---

A handful of situations push a refund past the standard 5-10 business day
window: the original payment method was closed or replaced (the refund
has to be manually redirected to a new method), the refund was flagged
for a routine fraud/compliance recheck, or the customer's bank is simply
slow to post the credit on their end.

If the original card has expired or been cancelled, most card networks
will still route the refund to the replacement card automatically, but
this can add several extra business days.

When a delay is caused by something on our side (a manual redirect or a
compliance recheck), the customer should be told plainly what is causing
the delay rather than just "please wait a few more days."
